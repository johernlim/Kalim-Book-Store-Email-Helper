Using the application

These terms describe use of Kalim BookStore Email Helper. By using the application, you agree to use it for lawful document sharing and only with Google accounts and files you are authorized to access.

Draft preparation and sending

The application creates a draft in the Google account you authorize, using the recipient, subject and files you select. It attempts to open the message in Gmail compose so you can review it, with a link to Gmail Drafts as a fallback. It does not send email automatically, confirm delivery, or guarantee that the recipient has received or read a message.

You are responsible for checking the sender, recipient, message and attachments before pressing Send in Gmail. Confirm you have permission to share the information in your documents.

Files and previews

The combined attachment limit is 20 MB. In-page previews support PDFs, images and text, subject to the browser and file format. Some formats, encrypted documents or damaged files cannot be previewed. A preview may differ from the original document; the original selected file is used for the attachment.

Appropriate use

Do not use the application for spam, phishing, malware, unlawful disclosure, or attempts to access another person's account without permission. Your use of Google and GitHub services is also subject to their applicable terms and policies.

Availability

The application depends on your browser, internet connection and Google's services. Errors, access restrictions or service interruptions may prevent draft creation. If a request is interrupted, check Gmail Drafts before trying again to avoid duplicates. Keep your original files; this application is not a document backup service.

Privacy

The Privacy Policy explains the data used for previews, Google authorization and Gmail draft creation, as well as how to remove access.

Contact and updates

For questions or support, contact kalimbookstore@gmail.com. Updates to these terms will be published here with an updated date.
