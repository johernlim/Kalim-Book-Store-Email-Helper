Privacy Policy

Kalim BookStore Email Helper
Last updated: 2 October 2026

What this application does

Kalim BookStore Email Helper lets you select and preview documents on your device, then create a Gmail draft with your chosen recipient, subject and attachments. You review and send the draft in Gmail.

Information used

The application processes the recipient email address and subject you enter, the names and contents of files you select, and the email address of the Google account you authorize. It obtains a temporary Google access token to identify that account and create the draft.

Google account permissions

We request your Google email address to identify the sender and the Gmail compose permission to create drafts with attachments. Google's compose permission also allows managing drafts and sending messages, but this application's code only creates drafts. It does not read your inbox, list existing messages, delete messages, or send email automatically.

How data is used and shared

File previews run in your browser. Selected documents are not sent to an external preview service. When you click Sent and authorize access, the recipient, subject, message body and attachments are transmitted directly from your browser to Google's Gmail API to create a draft in the authorized account.

The application does not send the draft to the recipient. You decide whether to send it in Gmail. Application data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models.

Storage and retention

This application has no database or backend for storing documents, recipient addresses, or Google tokens. Selected files and the temporary access token are held in the page's memory during use. The app does not persist them in cookies or browser storage. Reloading or closing the page clears its connection; your original files remain on your device.

A setup-complete flag is saved in your browser's local storage after a successful pop-up check, so future visits can open the form directly. This flag contains no email addresses, files or Google tokens. Clear this website's site data in your browser to remove it.

Drafts created in Gmail remain in your Google account until you send or delete them. Closing this website or revoking its access does not delete those drafts.

Hosting and Google services

GitHub Pages hosts the website and can process ordinary web-request information, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google provides authorization and Gmail services under the Google Privacy Policy. The application does not add analytics or advertising trackers.

Your choices

You can decline authorization, close the page without creating a draft, delete created drafts in Gmail, or remove this application's access through your Google Account connections. Removing access prevents further authorized use until you reconnect.

Google API Limited Use

Kalim BookStore Email Helper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact kalimbookstore@gmail.com. Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
