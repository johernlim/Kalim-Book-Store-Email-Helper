Before you start

Allow pop-ups for this site so Google sign-in can open. If already allowed, the form opens automatically. Otherwise, follow these steps and check again.

  1. If blocked, click the pop-up blocked icon in your browser's address bar.
  2. Choose Always allow pop-ups and redirects from this site, then click Done.
  3. Click Check pop-ups again to continue.

You can also use your browser's site settings to allow pop-ups and redirects. This check opens and closes a blank window without accessing Gmail. We remember successful setup in this browser so you can go straight to the form next time.

Choose one or more files. Maximum 20 MB in total.

    Click a filename to preview it here. PDF, image and text previews stay on this device.

    Connect your Google account when prompted. Keep this page open to reuse the connection. All selected files will be attached to a draft for you to review and send in Gmail.

    Your files upload directly to the Gmail account you authorize when you click Sent. This page does not store your documents.
